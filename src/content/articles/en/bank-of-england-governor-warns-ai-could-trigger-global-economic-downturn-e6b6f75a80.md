- Bailey warned that a collapse in AI sector growth could lead to a 'future market correction' spreading worldwide.
- He cited highly priced stock markets, increased investor borrowing, and concentration of money in major tech firms as factors that could amplify such a correction.

Bailey, speaking as FSB chair, warned G20 finance ministers that AI could cause a global economic downturn and pose significant cyber security risk to financial systems. He specifically noted that any collapse in AI sector growth could lead to a 'future market correction' spreading worldwide, amplified by highly priced stock markets, increased investor borrowing, and concentration of money in major tech firms. According to the BBC, these views were conveyed in an open letter to the G20, though the exact wording of the letter was not disclosed.
