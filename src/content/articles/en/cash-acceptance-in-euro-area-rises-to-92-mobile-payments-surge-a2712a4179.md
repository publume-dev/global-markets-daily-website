- In 2026, 92% of euro area companies with physical points of sale accept cash, up from 90% in 2024.
- Card payment acceptance remained stable at 88% between 2024 and 2026.
- Mobile payment acceptance among euro area companies rose sharply from 36% in 2024 to 68% in 2026.

The ECB's survey indicates that cash remains the most widely accepted payment method in the euro area, with its acceptance rate rebounding over two years. Meanwhile, the notable increase in mobile payment acceptance suggests rapid digital adoption, though transaction volumes or values are needed to fully assess its significance.

These trends may influence payment strategies for euro area businesses and consumer habits, but the extent of the impact remains to be evaluated with more detailed data such as transaction volumes.
