- Total assets of EU credit institutions: €34.33 trillion in March 2026 (€33.13 trillion in March 2025; +3.63% year on year).
- Aggregate non-performing loans ratio: 1.98% in March 2026, up 0.02 percentage points from a year earlier.
- Aggregate return on equity: 2.44% for Q1 2026, non-annualised.
- Aggregate Common Equity Tier 1 ratio: 16.27% in March 2026.
- EU aggregates are slightly affected because Q1 2026 Danish data were unavailable; Denmark is proxied by Q4 2025 stocks and Q1 2025 flows.
- Past data in the release have been revised, so earlier reference periods may differ from previously published figures.

Measured in euro terms, the year-on-year increase in aggregate assets was about €1.20 trillion (€34.33 trillion minus €33.13 trillion). The 0.02-percentage-point rise in the aggregate non-performing loan ratio suggests broadly stable asset quality at the aggregate level, though the Danish data proxy and historical revisions make period-to-period comparisons less precise. The return on equity is disclosed as non-annualised, so 2.44% is a first-quarter rate rather than a projected full-year return; the 16.27% CET1 ratio gives the capital context for the same aggregate balance sheet.
