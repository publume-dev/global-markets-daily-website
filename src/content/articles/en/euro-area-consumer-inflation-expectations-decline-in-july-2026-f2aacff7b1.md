- Median perceptions of inflation over the past 12 months decreased to 3.5% in July from 3.6% in June.
- Median expectations for inflation over the next 12 months decreased to 2.9% from 3.0%, and for three years ahead to 2.7% from 2.8%.
- Median expectations for inflation five years ahead remained unchanged at 2.4%.
- Expectations for nominal income growth over the next 12 months decreased to 1.0% from 1.1%, while nominal spending growth was unchanged at 3.6%.
- Expectations for economic growth over the next 12 months rose to -1.2% from -1.4%, while the expected unemployment rate in 12 months was unchanged at 11.2%.
- Expectations for mortgage interest rates in 12 months declined to 4.9% from 5.0%, while home price growth expectations were unchanged at 3.4%.

The decline in inflation expectations may reflect the pass-through of recent energy price falls, but the survey provides no specific reasons.
