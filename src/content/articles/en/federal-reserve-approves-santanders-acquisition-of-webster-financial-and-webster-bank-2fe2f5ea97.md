**Status**: Approved
**Applicants**: Banco Santander, S.A. and Santander Holdings USA, Inc.
**Direct target**: Webster Financial Corporation
**Indirect target**: Webster Bank, National Association
**Approving authority**: Federal Reserve Board

**Analysis**
The application structures the acquisition so that Santander Holdings USA, Inc. joins Banco Santander, S.A. as an applicant, and Webster Bank would be acquired indirectly through the takeover of Webster Financial Corporation. The Board's action is an approval of the application; the announcement does not indicate that the acquisition has been completed.
