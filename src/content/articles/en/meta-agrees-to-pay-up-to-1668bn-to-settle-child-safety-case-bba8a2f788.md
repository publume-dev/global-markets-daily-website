- Meta agrees to pay up to $16.68 billion to settle a court case with US states and territories over child safety on Facebook and Instagram.
- The settlement requires Meta to introduce rules for teenage users including daily limits and night-time blocks.
- Meta denied wrongdoing in agreeing to settle the case.

The settlement's magnitude and required measures could set a precedent for the social media industry, but Meta's denial means the settlement does not constitute an admission of liability. It remains unclear how these rules will be implemented technologically and whether they will apply globally or only to US users.
