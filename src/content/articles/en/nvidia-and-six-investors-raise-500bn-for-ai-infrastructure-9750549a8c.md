- Nvidia is working with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR to raise $500bn (£370bn) for AI infrastructure.
- The money will support Nvidia's own projects and those built by its partners, including new data centres and factories manufacturing AI chips.
- Companies using Nvidia's GPUs include Google, Meta, Amazon, Microsoft, SpaceX, Tesla, OpenAI and Anthropic.
- Those companies have collectively spent more than $1tn over three years on AI projects and infrastructure.
- The investors are, for the first time, treating AI hardware and infrastructure (“compute”) as an asset class.

According to the BBC report, the announcement positions AI compute as an investable asset class rather than a cost item for individual companies. Nvidia's list of GPU customers—Google, Meta, Amazon, Microsoft, SpaceX, Tesla, OpenAI and Anthropic—has collectively invested over $1tn in AI projects and infrastructure in three years, providing scale context for the new $500bn fund. The report does not specify how the $500bn is split by investor or over what period it will be raised.
