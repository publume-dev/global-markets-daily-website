- Shein aims for a market valuation of almost $27bn in its Hong Kong debut scheduled for 1 September.
- The company will offer nearly 280 million shares at a price range of HK$47.60 to HK$49.50 each, potentially raising $1.77bn.
- The target valuation is significantly lower than the $100bn valuation in 2022, reflecting weaker sales growth and higher costs.

The target valuation represents a sharp decline from the $100bn valuation in 2022, attributed to weaker sales growth and higher costs. The final IPO valuation and raise amount depend on the closing share price, which may vary within the stated range or beyond.
