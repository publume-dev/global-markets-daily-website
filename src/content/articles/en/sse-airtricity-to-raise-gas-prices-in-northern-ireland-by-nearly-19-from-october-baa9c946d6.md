- SSE Airtricity will raise gas prices by almost 19% from 1 October for about 200,000 domestic and small business customers in Northern Ireland.
- A typical household in the greater Belfast and west gas network areas will see an annual increase of nearly £172.
- The Utility Regulator attributes the rise to sustained wholesale gas prices, now 169 pence per therm, double pre-conflict levels.

The price hike directly affects roughly 200,000 households and small businesses in Northern Ireland, increasing energy costs for consumers. The Utility Regulator links the increase to sustained wholesale gas prices, now 169 pence per therm, twice pre-conflict levels. It remains unclear whether large industrial users face the same percentage increase.
