- Net loss: $238 million in Q2 2026, more than 10 times the loss in the same quarter a year earlier.
- Revenue: $1.7 million, up 89% year over year.
- Balance sheet: $2 billion in total assets, including roughly $1.9 billion in financial assets such as cash, short-term investments, and digital currencies.
- Strategy: The company says it will refocus on social media, including a service that provides faster access to market-moving posts from influential users; interim CEO Kevin McGurn says more than 10 customers have signed up.

Analyst Markus Thielen of 10x Research said the company is 'more of a crypto holdings firm wrapped around a media company,' and that the bulk of its losses came from that strategy. The BBC article attributes the swing partly to cryptocurrency declines but gives no specific split of the $238 million loss between digital-asset holdings and operations. Even with revenue up 89%, quarterly revenue of $1.7 million is far below the reported loss, underscoring how much the result depends on the value of its financial assets.

Watch whether Trump Media provides a fuller breakdown of the loss and whether the new subscription service for faster access to market-moving posts adds customers beyond the 'more than 10' cited by interim CEO Kevin McGurn; the refocusing statement is forward-looking.
