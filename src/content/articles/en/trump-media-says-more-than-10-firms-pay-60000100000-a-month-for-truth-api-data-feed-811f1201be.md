- More than 10 customers have signed up for Truth API, a service offering faster access to market-moving posts on Truth Social.
- Truth API launched at the beginning of August; interim CEO Kevin McGurn says it charges customers between $60,000 and $100,000 a month.
- Trump Media reported a net loss of $238 million for the quarter ended June 30, more than 10 times the year-earlier loss.
- Revenue was $1.7 million for the quarter, up 89% from a year earlier.

At the low end of the stated range, more than 10 subscribers would imply at least $660,000 a month ($7.9 million annualized) from Truth API, versus $1.7 million of reported quarterly revenue. That arithmetic is illustrative: the exact subscriber count and contract terms are not disclosed, so the service's actual revenue contribution remains uncertain. The earnings report also shows the loss widened sharply even as revenue grew.

Watch for Trump Media to disclose the number of Truth API subscribers and contract terms such as minimum commitments or duration; those details will determine the service's contribution to revenue.
