- The White House report says more than 40 countries have helped China dodge US tariffs by routing exports through nations with lower duties.
- The report names Canada, India, Mexico, Japan, and South Korea as countries that helped China evade tens of billions of dollars in tariffs.
- The White House estimates that between $30 billion and $300 billion in goods have been moved from countries with higher tariffs to those with lower rates.

The report's specific methodology and evidence have not been independently verified, but the estimated $30 billion to $300 billion range suggests a potentially significant impact on trade flows. If accurate, this could undermine the effectiveness of US tariffs and affect trade relations with the named countries.
